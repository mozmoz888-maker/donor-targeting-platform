# AI sessions

Using AI assistants (Cursor, Claude Code, Copilot, ChatGPT, and similar) is normal practice for
this project. If you want to reflect on that work with your mentor, keep notes or exports here.

- Any format is fine: exported markdown, JSON logs, screenshots, or a short summary per session.
- Redact secrets, tokens, and anything personal.
- A line or two on what worked and what you had to correct is more useful than a long dump.

Naming files by date or milestone helps (`session-01-api-sketch.md`, `2026-03-14-scoring.md`).

In the mentor walkthrough, be ready to discuss: what you delegated to the tool, how you checked
its output, and at least one place you overrode or fixed a suggestion.
